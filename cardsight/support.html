Statements

How do I add a statement?

Open the PDF in your bank's app or in Mail, tap Share and choose Cardsight. You can also tap + in Cardsight and pick a PDF, a screenshot or a scan. In the app, "Get your statement" has steps for your bank's app and email.

Which banks and statements work?

Credit card statements from any bank, as a PDF, screenshots or a scan. Menu names differ by bank, so look for Statements, then Download, PDF or Share. Password-protected PDFs can't be opened yet; add screenshots of the whole statement instead.

The total doesn't match my statement.

Cardsight checks every statement against its printed total and opens the review screen when something is off. Bonus or points lines that aren't money are kept apart under Card points. If it's still wrong, email us the bank name (not the statement) and we'll look into it.

Can I lock my device while a statement is read?

Yes. Reading continues in the background and its progress shows on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. If iOS stops it, it picks up the next time you open Cardsight.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 26.4 or later. Reading on Apple's Private Cloud Compute needs a device that supports Apple Intelligence; on other devices Cardsight asks before using our server, or reads on the device.

Premium and purchases

What's free, and what does Premium add?

Reading on your device and subscription tracking are always free, and the free plan includes 2 statements read in the cloud (reads that fail don't count). Premium adds unlimited cloud reading, every upcoming month in detail and CSV export.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Cardsight, go to Settings and, under Cardsight Premium, tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Cardsight Premium and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. If you're on the free trial and cancel, you won't be charged. Deleting the app doesn't cancel a subscription.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Can I share Premium with my family?

No, Cardsight Premium isn't shared through Family Sharing. Each Apple Account needs its own subscription.

Privacy and your data

Who reads my statement?

Apple's Private Cloud Compute first, which doesn't keep your data. Only if it can't, and only with your permission, our server reads the text, with your name and card numbers masked first. See the Privacy Policy.

Can I keep everything away from your server?

Yes. In Settings, under Reading, choose Apple only or This device only. Then nothing reaches us.

Where is my data, and how do I delete it?

On your device and in your private iCloud. To delete everything, go to Settings and, under Your data, tap Delete all data; it removes your statements, transactions, cards and corrections from this device and from iCloud.
